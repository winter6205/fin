# ACME Corporation - Corporate Travel and Expense Policy

Policy version 2026.1. Owner: Finance Operations. Scope: all employees on
approved business travel.

## 1. Working meals

Employees may claim up to 75 US dollars per person for a working business lunch and up to 110 US dollars per person for a working business dinner.

Breakfast purchased as part of a hotel stay is reimbursed at actual cost when the lodging receipt lists it separately.

## 2. Lodging

Lodging is reimbursed at actual cost up to 210 US dollars per night for domestic trips and up to 320 US dollars per night for international trips.

## 3. Receipts and submission

Any single expense above 25 US dollars requires an itemized receipt, submitted within 30 calendar days of the transaction date.

## 4. Approval

Expense reports must be approved by the traveler's direct manager before reimbursement is issued.

## 5. Exclusions

Personal entertainment, minibar charges, and clothing purchases are not reimbursable under this policy.
